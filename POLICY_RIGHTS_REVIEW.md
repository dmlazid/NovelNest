# NovelHaven content rights and AdSense review checklist

This is an internal checklist for the site owner, not a declaration that Google has approved any work. Do **not** upload contracts, personal details, copyright notices with private contact information, account credentials, or identity documents to this public GitHub repository.

## Review each novel and edition separately

For every novel and cover image currently published or considered for publication, privately record:

- Novel title and unique ID; original author and publisher / rights holder.
- The exact language and translation edition displayed on NovelHaven.
- How publication rights were obtained, from whom, and when.
- Whether the grant actually covers **republication of full chapter text on novelhaven.top**.
- Whether the grant separately covers **Google ads / commercial monetization**, derivative metadata, translations, and the displayed cover image.
- Permitted countries, relevant dates / expiration, revocation instructions, and chapter ranges.
- Where the evidence is kept securely outside this public repo, and how the rights holder can be contacted privately.
- Whether the text has been screened for prohibited sexual content, violence, hate and other Google Publisher Policy risks.
- What substantial, genuinely original value NovelHaven adds beyond reproducing chapters (e.g. original reviews, transparent comparisons, chapter navigation tools).
- Your review decision: eligible for public ad-supported display; ad-free/noindex; or remove/restrict. Do not treat unreviewed items as automatically cleared.

**A source listing or the statement `sourcewide_authorized: true` in an importer configuration is not independent proof that all novels, translations, artwork, and commercial uses are licensed.**

## Existing automated safeguards

- Duplicate chapter bodies have been removed from public chapter data without renumbering links.
- Hidden / restricted novels and duplicate chapter routes are ad-free and noindex in the compatible Pages output.
- New automatically discovered titles are held for human review under `scripts/auto_import_policy.json`.
- Legal and privacy utility pages should not request AdSense, but the homepage and substantive Reading Desk content retain publisher verification.

These improvements **do not** independently resolve copyright entitlement, unauthorized translations, adult-content risks, or Google's policy against monetizing replicated material without added value.

## Tasks that still need account-owner action

1. **Contact method published:** `novelhaven15@gmail.com` is listed on Contact, Copyright and Content Licensing pages for nonpublic rights inquiries. The site owner must confirm they can receive and respond to mail there; this deployment does not verify mailbox access or establish a secure document-upload system.
2. Review individual novel rights and covers using securely retained evidence, not public repo declarations.
3. Review Google AdSense Policy center and the rejection email for the exact enforcement item. Only an AdSense reviewer can clear it.
4. Configure a Google-certified CMP in your real AdSense account before serving personalized ads to EEA, UK and Switzerland visitors when required. NovelHaven's browser-data clear button is not a consent platform.
5. Check every ad-eligible URL for sufficient original usefulness and permitted content. Do not request a new review until the unresolved site-wide issues are addressed.
